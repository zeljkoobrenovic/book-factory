# Corvane — scenario ledger

Corvane is **fictional**. Every figure here is an illustration invented for the book, not data about any company. Every chapter's example must agree with this ledger. Extend it only by appending; never change a fact a written chapter already uses.

## The company

Corvane makes scheduling and dispatch software for field-service companies (heating, lifts, telecom installation): an office web application for planners, a mobile app for technicians, and integrations with customers' billing and stock systems. Customers pay per technician per month. Founded in 2018 in Rotterdam.

## Fixed numbers

- Stage 3 lead time for a change (from starting work on it to production): median 4 days before the hiring wave, 19 days after.
- Stage 3 Platform team: 5 engineers serving 11 product teams; 14 open launch requests at the peak.
