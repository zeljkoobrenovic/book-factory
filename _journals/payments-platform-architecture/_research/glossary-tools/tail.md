
## Where to Go Next

The definitions, the crosswalk and the index are three ways into one vocabulary. The reserve of the opening now has two rows: the book's own definition in Part III, and a crosswalk row that says which provider means what by the word. Treat a row that disagrees with its chapter as a defect to fix in one of them, and treat a crosswalk row as dated: before naming a column or a state after a provider's word, check that provider's current page and define your own neutral term first. The cards that put many of these terms to work are in [[toolkit]], and the map from each capability to its chapter is in [[domain-coverage]]. Every definition and every crosswalk row rests on a chapter and, through it, on a provider's documentation read on a date; those sources, each with its date and type, are listed in [[bibliography]], the last page of the book.
