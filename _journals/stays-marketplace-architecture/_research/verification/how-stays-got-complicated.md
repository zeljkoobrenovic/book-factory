## 5. Consistency with two-stay-marketplaces

- Booking.com 1996, Expedia 1996 as a Microsoft division, trivago 2005, HomeAway 2011 public filing, RFC 5545 2009, 5 May 2020 Airbnb message: consistent with chapter 01 (which gives October 1996, January 2005, IPO 2011).
- GDS and switches before the OTAs, agents on airline screens before 1977: consistent with Wave 1 (SABRE 1964, agent access 1976, THISCO 1989).
- Merchant model for US OTAs, agency model for Booking.com, facilitated payments for homes: consistent with Waves 2-4.
- **For the author to decide:** the page dates Airbnb to 2007 in Airbnb's own words ("born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests"), and its year table reads "2007 | Airbnb's first hosts welcome three guests"; chapter 01 Wave 4 says "AirBed & Breakfast launched in San Francisco on 11 August 2008 and became Airbnb in 2009". Not a strict contradiction (first hosting vs. site launch), and the page attributes the claim to the company, but a reader may see two dates. Options: keep both, or add "the site launched in 2008 ([[two-stay-marketplaces]])" to the page.
